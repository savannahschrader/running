About

Savannah Schrader

Savannah is a licensed massage therapist and RRCA running coach. Massage, for her, is nervous system work as much as muscle work: helping a stressed, overloaded, or training-tired body actually downshift, so recovery can happen.

She still coaches runners, and she uses movement and bodywork in her own life to stay healthy. That combination shows up on the table. Coaching taught her to read how someone moves, loads, and recovers. Bodywork is how she helps the system settle—listen first, work with what’s there, and leave you quieter than you arrived.

Approach

How Savannah works.

Sessions include a 60-minute relaxation massage, tailored to what you need that day. She starts with how you feel, then adjusts pressure, pace, and focus as she goes.

  • Regulation Unhurried work aimed at helping the nervous system settle, not just pressing on sore spots.
  • Recovery Support for training load, desk tension, and bodies that don’t get a full reset on their own.
  • Your pressure Light, firm, or in between. She would rather hear from you than guess.
  • Movement Savannah is a certified running coach and can help you build a plan if you want support with training, not only time on the table.

FAQ

Before you come in.

What is a session?

A 60-minute relaxation massage, tailored to what you need that day. New clients get $20 off the first session. Book online; the studio address comes with your confirmation.

I’ve never had a massage. What should I expect?

A short health history, a check-in on pressure and anything to skip, then the session. You are draped at all times. Talking after intake is optional.

What should I wear?

Whatever you are comfortable changing out of. You choose what stays on; only the area being massaged is uncovered.

Do I have to talk?

No. Speak up if something hurts, feels too light, or if the room isn’t comfortable. Quiet is completely fine.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice so that time can go to someone else.
