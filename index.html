Coaching

In-person run coaching.

Savannah meets you in Bridle Trails for the session—on the roads, the trails, or wherever you train—and can write a plan for a 5K, 10K, half, or marathon around your life, your terrain, and your body.

  • Accountability Showing up is the hardest part. Give yourself a better shot at following through with your run training by having me come meet you for an in-person run session at your desired place and time.
  • 1-on-1 No need to rush home from work to make the local group run on time. As your personal run coach, you can count on me to meet you in Bridle Trails and the surrounding area at any time that works for you.
  • Safety Running alone, especially on a dark, early morning, can be nerve wracking. Let me take the stress away and be your on demand run buddy.
  • Results Pushing yourself hard on a workout (like hills for example) can be much harder when you’re solo. I’ll run with and coach you through any type of run workout, at any level of intensity, specific to your fitness or race goal.
  • Not a runner yet? I will meet you for walks, run/walks, light jogs, or anything you feel ready for, as you begin your running journey. Say goodbye to shame, embarrassment, or worry about getting through a whole workout alone. I will come work out alongside you and make sure you’re not doing too much too soon.
  • Form correction Whether you’ve seen a horrendous finish line photo of yourself, experience regular knee pain every time you hit mile 3, or simply HATE running, I will ensure you’re moving and breathing correctly with drills, cues and video example.
  • 5K, 10K, half, and marathon plans Season-long training for the race you’re pointing toward—a first 5K, a faster 10K, a half, or marathon—written around the week you actually have, with load and recovery treated as one system.
