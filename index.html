Approach

How Savannah works.

There is no script for a “perfect” massage. Savannah starts with how you feel today, then adjusts pressure, pace, and focus as she goes. Speak up anytime—too much, too little, too warm, too quiet. The session is yours.

  • Wellness Slow, settling work for the nervous system. An hour to downshift when life has been loud.
  • Recovery Support for sore, overworked, or training-tired bodies. Tell Savannah what’s been bothering you.
  • Your pressure Light, firm, or somewhere in between. She would rather hear from you than guess.
  • Always draped Only the area being worked is undraped. You decide what you wear underneath. Comfort comes first.

FAQ

Before you come in.

How do I book?

Use Book a session to pick a time. The studio address in Bridle Trails comes with your confirmation.

Where is the studio?

Summit Bodywork is in Bridle Trails, Bellevue. For privacy, the exact address is shared after you book.

I’ve never had a massage. What should I expect?

You’ll complete a short health history and meet Savannah in the treatment room. She’ll ask about pressure and anything that shouldn’t be worked. You are draped at all times. Talking is optional after that intake.

What should I wear?

Wear whatever you feel comfortable changing out of. During the session you choose what stays on; you’ll be fully draped either way. Only the area being massaged is uncovered.

Do I have to talk during the session?

No. Savannah will check in on pressure at the start. After that, quiet is completely fine. Do speak up if something hurts, feels too light, or if the room isn’t comfortable.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice so that time can go to someone else.
