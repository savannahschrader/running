Coaching

In-person run coaching.

This is in-person run coaching. Savannah meets you for the session—on the roads, the trails, or wherever you train—and builds the work around your life, your terrain, and your body.

  • Accountability Showing up is the hardest part. Give yourself a better shot at following through with your run training by having us come meet you for an in-person run session at your desired place and time.
  • 1-on-1 No need to rush home from work to make the local group run on time. As your personal run coach, you can count on us to meet you in Bridle Trails and the surrounding area at any time that works for you.
  • Safety Running alone, especially on a dark, early morning, can be nerve wracking. Let us take the stress away and be your on demand run buddy.
  • Results Pushing yourself hard on a workout (like hills for example) can be much harder when you’re solo. We’ll run with and coach you through any type of run workout, at any level of intensity, specific to your fitness or race goal.
  • Not a runner yet? We will meet you for walks, run/walks, light jogs, or anything you feel ready for, as you begin your running journey. Say goodbye to shame, embarrassment, or worry about getting through a whole workout alone. We will come work out alongside you and make sure you’re not doing too much too soon.
  • Form correction Whether you’ve seen a horrendous finish line photo of yourself, experience regular knee pain every time you hit mile 3, or simply HATE running, we will ensure you’re moving and breathing correctly with drills, cues and video example.

Training plans

Plans for 5K, 10K, half, and marathon.

Season-long training written around your life, your terrain, and the race you’re pointing toward—whether that’s your first 5K or 26.2.

  • 5K A first race or a faster finish. Form, easy running, and a plan you can keep.
  • 10K More volume with the same patience. Build from a 5K base without jumping ahead.
  • Half marathon Longer days, smarter recovery, and work that respects the week you actually have.
  • Marathon Season-long work for 26.2, with load and recovery treated as one system.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is in-person run coaching?

Savannah meets you for the session in Bridle Trails and the surrounding area. It’s 1-on-1 coaching on the roads or trails—accountability, safety, form, and workouts specific to your goal.

Do you write training plans?

Yes. Plans cover 5K, 10K, half marathon, and marathon, written around your life and the race you’re pointing toward.

Where do sessions happen?

In-person run coaching is based in Bridle Trails. Savannah can meet you there and in the surrounding area at a time that works for you.

How do I book a massage?

Massage is for wellness and recovery and is booked at the spa. Use the massage section to go to booking, or ask Savannah if you aren’t sure where to start.

How do I get started?

Coaching begins with a free 15-minute consultation. Use Book a session or send a note through the contact form.
